## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Import Early** — Import transactions monthly to avoid year-end reconciliation nightmare with 10,000+ transactions
- **Tag Wallets** — Label wallets by purpose (trading, holding, DeFi) for cleaner portfolio tracking and reporting
- **Cost Basis Method** — Use HIFO (Highest In First Out) to minimize capital gains vs FIFO for lower tax liability
- **DeFi Gas Fees** — Track gas fees separately—they reduce taxable gains when selling crypto used for gas
- **Staking Classification** — Consult CPA whether to report staking as income at receipt or at sale (IRS unclear)
